Common tasks & FAQ

Short answers for what people most often want to do. For something broken, see Troubleshooting.

Use one device for several accounts

  1. Pair each phone with the device in Pokémon GO, one phone at a time. See Pair with Pokémon GO.
  2. Open the app. Each phone gets its own card under Device Profiles.
  3. Name each phone in Known devices to tell the accounts apart.

The device holds up to 4 connections, and the companion app uses one of them. Each phone keeps its own Autospin and Autocatch settings.

Move a device to a new phone

  1. In the app, open Known devices and tap Forget on the old phone. Disconnect it from the device first.
  2. On the old phone, remove the device from Bluetooth settings.
  3. On the new phone, install the companion app and connect to the device. See Connect the companion app.
  4. Pair the device with Pokémon GO on the new phone.

The new phone starts with fresh counts. The old phone's counts go away when you forget it.

Keep the game connected

Pokémon GO reconnects by itself when the device and phone are in range and Bluetooth is on. Two tools help when it does not:

  • The companion app can alert you when the game reports a problem. See Game monitor.
  • PoGo+LE is a separate Android app. It skips Android's pairing dialog and alerts you when the connection drops.

If connections drop often, see Frequent disconnects.

What the light means

The light blinks slowly while the device waits for a phone. It blinks 1 to 4 times, then pauses, for each connected Pokémon GO phone. Fast blinking means the device needs its secrets. Three long flashes after power-on mean it restarted after a crash. The full list is in Status light.

Is it safe for my account?

PGPemu is not an official product, and Niantic does not approve it. Pokémon GO's rules forbid unofficial accessories and automation, so Niantic can warn, suspend, or ban an account that uses it. Autospin and Autocatch add to that risk. Use it at your own risk, and never on an account you cannot lose.

The companion app collects no personal data. See Privacy.

Which phones work?

PGPemu works with Android phones. Bluetooth behavior differs between makers and Android versions, so some phones pair or reconnect less reliably than others. The PoGo+LE project keeps a device compatibility table in its repository. Check it before you buy a phone for this.
