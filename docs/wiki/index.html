pgpemu

PGPemu turns a small piece of hardware into a Pokémon GO Plus accessory. It pairs with Pokémon GO over Bluetooth the same way a real Poké Ball Plus or GO Plus does, then spins Pokéstops and catches Pokémon on your behalf while your phone stays in your pocket.

A companion Android app handles everything else: pairing, per-device settings, and diagnostics, all over Bluetooth Low Energy.

What you need

  • A PGPemu device

    A powered-on PGPemu accessory, already set up with its own pairing identity. If you don't have one yet, see the project page for what hardware is needed and how to build one.

  • PGPemu Companion

    The Android app used to pair with the device, check its status, and change its settings. Requires Android 12 or newer and Bluetooth Low Energy support.

Install the companion app

The app isn't on the Play Store yet. Build it from the source repository's companion-app project in Android Studio and install it to your phone, or check GitHub Releases for a prebuilt APK if one is available.

Start here

Once the app is installed and your device is powered on, head to Connect & Pair to link the app to your device and pair the device with Pokémon GO.
