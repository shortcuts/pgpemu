Changelog

What's new in each release. See GitHub Releases for the full history.

Unreleased

  • New: the app connects to your last device when it opens and reconnects after a dropped connection.
  • New: the app tells you once a day when a newer release exists for the app or the device firmware.
  • New: update the device firmware from the app over Bluetooth. Hold the board button for 3 seconds to allow it. The device goes back to the old firmware if the new one does not start. Holding the button for 3 to 9 seconds no longer turns Advertising on or off.
  • New: Forget all pairings in the Danger Zone clears every pairing and setting but keeps the device's identity. Holding the board button for 10 seconds does the same without the app. The app needs the matching firmware version.
  • New: a device with no secrets now starts in setup mode, and the app can import your secrets file over Bluetooth. Before, it stayed silent until a USB reflash. The app needs the matching firmware version.
  • Changes made from the app now apply the value you picked, so a repeated tap or a second phone cannot undo it. The app needs the matching firmware version.
  • New: the app shows an About device card with the firmware version and health details, and asks you to update firmware that is too old.
  • Fixed Advertising switching itself back on after you turned it off in the app, and after a device disconnected.
  • New: the Status card shows whether the device is currently sending out its Bluetooth signal.
  • On Android 16 and newer, the app now repairs a broken pairing by itself.
  • The app now shows errors while connected, in plain language with the error code in brackets.
  • Settings now save the moment you change them, and the Save to device button is gone. The app needs the matching firmware version.
  • Updating the device firmware now keeps your pairings. The first update to this version needs one USB reflash, and you pair Pokémon GO once more.
  • The device now keeps retrying to start Advertising if the first attempts fail.

1.0.1 — February 18, 2026

  • Fixed a stall that could leave a connection queue stuck.
  • Fixed a crash that could occur during device handshakes.

1.0.0 — February 1, 2026

  • New: per-device settings, so each connected Pokémon GO account keeps its own Autospin and Autocatch state.
  • New: caught, fled, and spin counters shown per device.
  • New: log levels, for more or less detail when diagnosing a problem.
  • Autospin now pauses automatically when the item bag is full, and resumes on its own once there's room.
  • Reconnecting to a previously paired account is now faster and more reliable.
  • Settings are restored automatically on every new connection.
