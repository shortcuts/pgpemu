Pairing assistant

The pairing assistant taps Pair on your phone's pairing prompt for the device. Without it, you tap Pair yourself.

Skip the pairing prompt

The first time you connect, your phone asks you to pair with Pokemon GO Plus. The pairing assistant taps Pair for you. Switch on Skip the pairing prompt below the Scan button, read the notice, and tap Continue. Your phone then opens its Accessibility settings. Find PGP Companion pairing assistant and switch it on. If your phone says the setting is restricted, open the app's info, tap the three dots, choose Allow restricted settings, and try again. If it still does not work, see The pairing prompt still shows.

Rooted phones

If your phone is rooted, switch on Pair silently with root below the Scan button, or in the app's Settings tab. Allow root access when your phone asks. After that, the pairing prompt never shows.
