Connect the companion app

Power on the PGPemu device, open PGPemu Companion, and tap Scan for devices. Every nearby device appears in a list as it's found, with its Bluetooth address and signal strength. If you have more than one nearby, the strongest signal is usually the closest. Tap yours in the list. The app connects and completes pairing on its own — there's no PIN or passphrase to type.

  • If the scan ends with nothing in the list, the app says No devices found. Check the device is powered on and nearby, then tap Scan again.
  • The status pill in the top bar shows Scanning while it looks, then Connecting, then Bonding as pairing goes through.
  • Once pairing finishes, the pill switches to Ready and the app shows the full dashboard: status, device profiles, settings, diagnostics, and the danger zone.
  • The first time you connect, your phone may ask for Bluetooth permission. Allow it — without it, the app can't scan for the device.
  • On Android 16 or newer, the phone also asks once to pair the device with the app. Allow it, so the app can repair a broken pairing by itself.
  • Later connections to a device you've already paired are quicker, since pairing only needs to happen once.
  • The app remembers the last device you connected to. When you open the app, it connects to that device by itself if it is in range. If the connection drops, the app tries again for about two minutes. Disconnect stops this.

Set up a device with no secrets

A device that has no secrets, or one you reset, shows up in the scan as PGPemu Setup. Pokémon GO does not see it. Connect to it in the app like any other device. The app then shows Set up device instead of the dashboard.

  1. Tap Import secrets.csv and pick your secrets file. If the file is not valid, the app says so and sends nothing.
  2. The device saves the secrets and restarts by itself.
  3. The device now has a new Bluetooth address. Scan again and connect to it, then pair it with Pokémon GO.

Warning: until you import the file, anyone in Bluetooth range can set up this device. Do it in a place where nobody else is nearby.

Pair with Pokémon GO

This is a separate step from connecting the companion app, done from inside Pokémon GO itself, the same way you'd pair a real Poké Ball Plus or GO Plus.

  1. In Pokémon GO, open Settings and go to the Pokémon GO Plus / Poké Ball Plus pairing screen.
  2. Start the search. Your PGPemu device shows up in the scan like a real accessory.
  3. Select it. Pairing completes automatically, with no passphrase required.

Once paired, Pokémon GO reconnects to it automatically whenever both are in range and Bluetooth is on — the same as it would with genuine hardware.

Multiple connections

A single PGPemu device can hold up to 4 simultaneous Bluetooth connections at once, shared between Pokémon GO and the companion app. Each connection takes one slot, and the companion app takes a slot too. Each slot has its own device profile (numbered #0 to #3) with independent Autospin and Autocatch settings — see Device Profiles.

This is useful if more than one Pokémon GO account uses the same device, since each account's connection is tracked and configured separately. The Settings page lets you lower the connection limit below 4 if you'd rather keep it reserved for fewer accounts.

Disconnecting

Tap Disconnect at the bottom of the companion app screen to end its own connection. This doesn't affect Pokémon GO's connection to the device — the two are independent. To drop every connection at once, including Pokémon GO's, use Disconnect all clients in Diagnostics.
