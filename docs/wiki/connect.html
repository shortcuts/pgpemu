Connect and pair

Connect the companion app first, then pair the device with Pokémon GO. A device with no secrets needs setup first.

Connect the companion app

Power on the PGPemu device, open PGPemu Companion, and tap Scan for devices. Every nearby device appears in a list as it's found, with its Bluetooth address and signal strength. If you have more than one nearby, the strongest signal is usually the closest. Tap yours in the list. The app connects and completes pairing on its own. You type no PIN or passphrase.

  • If the scan ends with nothing in the list, the app says No devices found. Check the device is powered on and nearby, then tap Scan again.
  • The status pill in the top bar shows Scanning while it looks, then Connecting, then Bonding as pairing goes through.
  • Once pairing finishes, the pill switches to Ready and the app shows four tabs at the bottom: Dashboard (status, device profiles, activity), Settings, Diagnostics (with the danger zone), and About.
  • The first time you connect, your phone may ask for Bluetooth permission. Allow it. Without it, the app cannot scan for the device.
  • On Android 16 or newer, the phone also asks once to pair the device with the app. Allow it, so the app can repair a broken pairing by itself.
  • Later connections to a paired device are quicker, because pairing happens once.
  • The app lets go of the device when you leave it, so Pokémon GO can connect. When you open the app again, tap Scan and pick the device. If the connection drops while the app is open, the app tries again for about two minutes. Disconnect stops this.

To skip the pairing prompt, use the pairing assistant.

Pair with Pokémon GO

This step is separate from connecting the companion app. You do it in Pokémon GO, as you would pair a real Poké Ball Plus or GO Plus.

  1. In Pokémon GO, open Settings and go to the Pokémon GO Plus / Poké Ball Plus pairing screen.
  2. Start the search. Your PGPemu device shows up in the scan like a real accessory.
  3. Select it. Pairing completes automatically, with no passphrase required.

Once paired, Pokémon GO reconnects automatically when both are in range and Bluetooth is on.

Disconnecting

Tap Disconnect at the bottom of the Dashboard tab to end its own connection. This does not affect Pokémon GO's connection to the device. To drop every connection at once, including Pokémon GO's, use Disconnect all clients in Diagnostics.
