Device profiles

The Device Profiles section shows one card per connected phone, numbered 1 to 4. A card shows the phone's name and the end of its address. A phone without a name shows as Profile and its number. Name a phone in Known devices to tell your accounts apart. Whichever Pokémon GO account is connected to a given slot is controlled by that card's settings.

  • Each card has a SPIN dot and a CATCH dot. Tap SPIN to turn it on or off for that slot. Tap CATCH to step through off, new, and all.
  • A lit, colored dot means the behavior is on. A dim dot means it's off.
  • Underneath, three lines show how many Pokémon you Caught, how many Fled, and how many Pokéstops you Spun since the device last restarted.

Warnings

A profile shows Bag full, Box full, or No balls in red when Pokémon GO reports that problem. No balls also appears when the Pokéstop is out of range. The note goes away when the game shows its next normal signal, such as a Pokémon or Pokéstop in range. The device keeps the note, so you see it after you open the app. The device and the app need matching versions.

Activity

The Activity card lists what happens in Pokémon GO as it happens, newest first: a Pokémon caught or fled, items received, a full bag or box, no Poké Balls left, and Pokémon GO connecting or disconnecting. Each line shows the time and the profile number. The list keeps the last 50 events and starts empty each time you connect. Tap Clear to empty it. The device and the app need matching versions; with an older device the card does not appear.

Known phones

The Known devices card lists every phone that has connected to Pokémon GO through the device, the most recent first. A phone you are using right now shows Connected.

  • Tap Rename to give a phone a name you recognize. The name can have up to 15 letters, digits or symbols.
  • Tap Forget to remove a phone. The device erases its pairing and settings. The next time that phone connects, it pairs again from scratch. You cannot forget a phone while it is connected.
  • Under each phone, All time counts what Pokémon GO did through the device: Pokémon caught and fled, Pokéstops spun, full bag, full box, and out of balls or out of range of a Pokéstop. The counts survive a restart of the device. While the phone is connected, This connection shows the counts since it connected. The numbers update when the app connects and after a reset, so reconnect the app to see newer counts.
  • Tap Reset stats to set the all-time counts of one phone back to zero. Forget all pairings in the Danger Zone and forgetting a phone erase its counts too.
  • The counts need the matching firmware version. If the device loses power, the last few events can be missing.
  • The list holds 15 phones. When a 16th connects, the device drops the one used longest ago.
  • A phone that paired before you updated shows up after its next connection.

Autospin

When Autospin is on, the device automatically presses its button whenever Pokémon GO signals that you're near a Pokéstop or Gym you can spin. You don't have to touch your phone — walk past a stop and it spins on its own.

Autocatch

When Autocatch is on, the device automatically presses its button when Pokémon GO signals a wild Pokémon encounter, the same way pressing the button on a real GO Plus registers a catch attempt.

Autocatch has three modes. The CATCH dot on the card shows which one is active.

  • CATCH off — the device never presses for a wild Pokémon.
  • CATCH new — the device presses only for a Pokémon you have not caught yet, which saves Poké Balls.
  • CATCH all — the device presses for every wild Pokémon.

CATCH new needs a device updated to the matching firmware version. An older device only offers off and all.

How it decides when to press

Pokémon GO Plus accessories, including PGPemu, take their cue from a pattern of light the game sends over the connection rather than anything shown on your screen. PGPemu recognizes each of these patterns and reacts accordingly:

  • Pokémon caught — the button press succeeded.
  • Pokémon fled — the encounter ended without a catch.
  • Pokéstop or Gym spun.
  • Item bag is full.
  • Pokémon box is full.
  • Poké Balls are empty.

The device does not press the button for the last three patterns. It keeps Autospin and Autocatch turned on and does not pause or resume them. Clear space or restock in the game.

Button presses use small randomized delays rather than firing instantly, closer to how a person reacts than a fixed, mechanical timing.

Settings stick around

The device remembers Autospin and the Autocatch mode for each Pokémon GO account and keeps them across reconnects and restarts. An account the device has never seen before starts with Autospin on and Autocatch set to all.
